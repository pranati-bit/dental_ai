Do these teeth need braces?

Upload a frontal photo of teeth. A small neural network (built from scratch in NumPy) estimates whether braces are likely needed. Everything runs in your browser; the photo is not uploaded.

Research demo, not a diagnosis. The model was trained on 192 patients and scored about two thirds correct on 39 unseen patients. The labels come from our own rule for counting rotated and misaligned front teeth. Ask a dentist or orthodontist for a real assessment.

What the model sees

Teeth area, grayscale, contrast-equalized, resized to 144 × 64.

Prediction

How well does it work?

Rows: the true label. Columns: what the model predicted. Chance level is 33%.

How it works

  • Crop to the teeth area, convert to grayscale, and equalize contrast.
  • Principal component analysis (PCA) turns the 9,216 pixel values into 117 numbers.
  • A neural network with two hidden layers (16 and 8 units) outputs three probabilities.

Labels: frontal teeth with 0 rotated or misaligned teeth are "no need", 1 or 2 are "borderline", and 3 or more are "braces likely". Patients who already wear appliances were excluded.
